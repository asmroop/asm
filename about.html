Who We Are

The inception of ASMROOP was not merely driven by the thought of starting a business; it was born from a deep understanding of a major gap within the salon and beauty industry.

We observed that across numerous cities and towns in India, countless salon and beauty professionals deliver exceptional work. However, due to the lack of an established brand presence, they often fail to receive the recognition and value commensurate with their services. On the other hand, customers face the constant challenge of searching for quality salons and trusted artists whenever they travel to a new city or town.

In the realm of beauty and makeovers, people naturally tend to trust only those salons and artists whose work they have experienced firsthand. Yet, when visiting a different city or town, they are forced to scout for new locations and unfamiliar professionals. This process consumes time and leaves a lingering uncertainty regarding the quality of service.

ASMROOP was conceived as a definitive solution to this very challenge.

Our objective is to build a pan-India network of premier salons, beauty studios, makeovers, and fashion hubs where customers receive a consistent standard of service quality, training, and overall experience at every outlet. We envision a future where individuals traveling to any city or town never have to wonder, “Where is the best salon?” Instead, they can place their absolute trust solely in ASMROOP.

The ASMROOP franchise model is deeply rooted in this philosophy. Our primary goal is not to establish new greenfield salons from scratch, but rather to partner with existing, successfully operating salons and beauty parlours, integrating them into the ASMROOP network. This empowers local business owners with a strong brand identity while guaranteeing customers reliable, top-tier services.

We believe that a resilient brand is built on mutual accountability toward both its franchise partners and its customers. Therefore, ASMROOP franchises are not granted for a lifetime; instead, they are subject to periodic renewals based strictly on performance, service quality, customer satisfaction, and adherence to core brand standards.

Our ultimate dream is to expand this network to every district, city, and town across India, establishing ASMROOP as the ultimate benchmark of trust, quality, and consistent experience in salon and beauty services.
